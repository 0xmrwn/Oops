Oops is free and open source, under the MIT license. There is no Oops account and no subscription: it works with an AI you already have.

MIT license · signed and notarized · automatic updates

Anywhere you type.

Mail, chat, notes, documents, and the text boxes in your browser. It stays out of terminals and password fields on purpose.

Tested in 254 editor setups: 15 web editors × 5 browser engines, 11 native toolkits

It is built not to.

Oops checks every change before applying it, and keeps code, links, mentions and line breaks exactly as you wrote them. When it is not sure, it changes nothing. Press the shortcut again within five minutes and, if you have not typed since, your original is back.

Edit-by-edit validation · rewrites rejected · restore for 5 minutes

Only the text you act on.

It goes straight to the AI you chose, with no Oops server in between. Dictation is transcribed on your Mac unless you pick an online option. Oops looks at the field you are in only when you press the shortcut.

Direct HTTPS requests · keys in the macOS Keychain · statistics stay on the Mac

Yours.

Sign in with ChatGPT, use Claude Code, add an API key, or run a model on your own Mac. Oops asks it for small corrections, not a rewrite.

ChatGPT · Claude Code · OpenAI API · any OpenAI-compatible server

Hold, talk, release.

What you say is written where your cursor is. By default the speech-to-text model runs on your Mac, so your voice is not sent anywhere, and the recording is never kept.
