How to read this report

Correction policy and grading labels

Quality

Necessary valid edits earn credit. Optional edits earn no correction credit and count as excess changes in minimal-edit precision. Harm is reported separately. A valid alternative can fix an error without matching the reference.

Minimal-edit F0.5 weights precision more than recall. It is a custom policy score, not an ERRANT score. Unresolved judgments stay unresolved.

Time and reliability

Completion time measures a full answer on the final send. Full wait includes provider pacing, failed sends and retries. First token is diagnostic; Oops needs the complete correction.

Raw-model preservation failures and dropped edits remain visible even when reconciliation produces safe pasted text.

Confidence intervals resample case families, keeping repeated attempts together. Small samples and unfinished judging limit what can be concluded. Judge agreement is provisional until validated against human output labels.
